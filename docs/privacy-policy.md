# Privacy policy

The policy takes effect on November 12, 2026.
Yasunobu Sakashita develops the app.
This policy covers the iPhone app named `物件管理くん`.

## Records and attachments

The app stores property and contact details in its private storage on your device.
Private storage also holds contracts, repair records, photographs, and documents.
The app doesn't send those records or attachments to the developer or an app server.
The app has no account, advertising, tracking, or analytics service.
The developer doesn't collect data through the app or share app records with third parties.

## System permissions and sharing

The app requests camera access when you choose to take a photograph for a record.
The system photo picker provides only the photographs you select.
The app requests local notification permission when you enable a reminder.
Reminder titles can appear on your device's notification screen according to your system settings.
You can change camera and notification permissions in Settings.
Declining permission doesn't prevent saving property records.
If you share a document through a system viewer, you choose its destination.
The system sends the selected document to that destination.

## Retention, deletion, and backups

Records remain in the app's device storage until you remove the app or erase the device.
Offloading the app preserves its records.
Apple's device backup services may include app records according to your backup settings.
Manage and delete device backups through Apple's system settings.
The developer can't retrieve or delete records stored on your device or in your backups.

## Support and changes

Contact [hi@sakashita.email](mailto:hi@sakashita.email) for app support or privacy questions.
Sending a support message shares the information you choose to include with the developer.
The app doesn't automatically attach records, photographs, or diagnostic logs to support messages.
The developer uses support messages to answer the inquiry.
Ask the developer to delete support correspondence by emailing the same address.
The developer updates this policy when data handling changes.
The App Store privacy information also reflects the changes before release.
